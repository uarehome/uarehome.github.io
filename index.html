You Are Home uploader

You Are Home is a YouTube channel of classical music played over slowly moving painted rooms, for reading, studying and quiet evenings.

You Are Home uploader is the channel's own private tool. Through the YouTube API Services, it:

  • uploads and schedules the channel's videos and Shorts on the You Are Home channel;
  • reads the channel's own analytics (views, watch time, subscribers) so the channel's owner can see what works.

Only the channel's owner uses it, on their own YouTube account. It offers no sign-up, no accounts and no service to anyone else.
